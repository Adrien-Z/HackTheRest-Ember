## Campaign direction

- Brand idea: Sleep is a skill, and EMBER is the coach.
- Visual language: near-black midnight space, electric brand blue, tactile checked bedding, frosted light, and the Blue Box character.
- Primary palette: `#05060B`, `#1F6EFF`, `#162C60`, pale periwinkle, and restrained warm-cream highlights.
- Typography direction: modern sans serif, compact uppercase `EMBER`, large editorial headlines, and generous negative space.
- Voice: calm, intelligent, reassuring, and evidence-aware.
